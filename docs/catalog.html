Bro (default) player

The player character (Rambo look). Stands one and a half blocks tall (24 px); holding Crouch makes him exactly one block tall (16 px) and slower, so he fits through a single destroyed block. Runs, jumps, sprays rifle fire; dies after max_hp hits (bullets, or blasts which take several hearts at once). Each death costs one run life and restarts the current level (design/run.yaml). Every future Bro copies this file and changes what makes them unique.

Mook (enemy soldier) enemy

Masked soldier. Patrols back and forth and turns at walls and ledges. He only sees forward — if the Bro is in front of him, within vision range, roughly at his height, with no blocks in between, he shows "!", waits reaction_time, then fires bursts. After losing sight of the Bro he keeps watching for forget_time, then patrols again. Shot from behind, he turns around. Every hit sprays blood; when he dies he bursts, falls, and his body stays for the rest of the level in a spreading pool of blood (explosions still toss bodies around).

Kamikaze (suicide bomber) enemy

Wears a dynamite vest and holds a lit stick. Patrols like a mook and sees the same way (forward only, no blocks in between). When he spots the Bro he screams and sprints straight at him, jumping over low walls. When he gets within trigger_range he blows up — killing himself and, at full blast, the Bro too. Shooting him is the answer, but he also explodes when he dies (after death_fuse), so kill him far away. His blast breaks blocks, sets off barrels and kills other enemies too. Leaves no body — only blood.

Terrain block terrain

16×16 solid tile that levels are built from. Bullets (player and enemy) chip it; at 0 HP it bursts into debris and is gone for good. Any block whose center is inside an explosion is destroyed at once. Blocks with open sky above (grass_clearance air tiles) automatically become grass with hanging strands; tunnel and cave floors stay bare earth. Each block's variant is picked from its map position, so the same level always looks the same.

Explosive barrel hazard

Red drum, one block. A hit from any bullet (or a nearby blast) sets it burning; after fuse_time it explodes. Barrels that touch each other (side by side, stacked or diagonal) explode together as ONE bigger blast — radius and damage grow with every extra barrel, and it looks different (mushroom fireball + shockwave instead of a small blast). Barrels further away that are inside the blast catch fire and go off a moment later (chain reaction). Solid — you can stand on it, and it falls if the ground under it is destroyed.

Explosion effect

Everything inside the radius is hit at once — blocks are destroyed (so a blast always opens a hole several blocks wide), enemies take blast damage, the Bro loses hearts, barrels catch fire, and bodies are tossed. One source (a single barrel or a kamikaze) uses one of three small blasts (picked from its map position, so the same barrel always looks the same). A cluster of touching barrels instead plays a mushroom fireball plus a ground shockwave scaled to the cluster, with a small blast on every barrel in it, more smoke, more fire and a harder screen shake.

Run loop

Source: design/run.yaml — in code: GameData.RUN

A run is the levels below played in order. Each level ends at the extraction flag (X), but the flag only opens after you have killed the level's kill_quota enemies (any enemy, any way — bullets, barrels, kamikaze blasts all count). Touch the open flag to clear the level. After the last level the run loops back to level 1 and the loop counter goes up. Dying costs one life and restarts the current level (kills reset); losing the last life ends the run and you start again from level 1 with full lives.

lives3 livesLives at the start of a run. Each death costs one and restarts the current level.

run_over_delay3.0 sHow long "RUN OVER" shows after the last life is lost, before a new run starts.

Storyboards

Bro — moveset

The beats every Bro must sell. New Bros copy this board and change only what is unique to them.

1. Ready
Stands his ground, rifle up, bandana fluttering. Always looks ready to fight.

2. Run
Fast, forward-leaning sprint. Body bobs on each step, gun never lowers.
sfx: footstep

3. Jump
Tucked legs, bandana flips up. Jump is high and snappy, not floaty.
sfx: jump

4. Shoot
Gun kicks back 1px each shot, muzzle flash at the tip. Hold to spray.
sfx: shoot

5. Crouch
Hold Crouch (S / Down) and the Bro drops from one and a half blocks to exactly one. He moves slower but can still shoot, and he fits through a one-block tunnel. Under a low ceiling he stays down until there is room to stand.

6. Destroy
Bullets chew through dirt. Three hits and a block bursts into debris. Standing he is 1.5 blocks tall, so a single destroyed block is a tunnel only a crouching Bro fits through.
sfx: block_break

7. Spotted
A mook only sees forward. Step in front of him, near his height, with nothing in between, and a "!" pops up — you have half a second.
sfx: enemy_alert

8. Firefight
He fires slow red bursts. Jump over them, or break the block under him. Two hits and he drops.
sfx: enemy_shoot

9. Hit
Each enemy hit costs a heart and makes the Bro blink (briefly invulnerable). Lose all three and you lose a life; the level restarts. Blasts take two or three hearts at once and throw you.
sfx: player_hurt

Run loop (roguelike)

What one run of the game feels like. This is the design target every feature serves.

🚁

1. Drop in
Helicopter drops a random Bro into a procedurally built jungle level.

2. Blast through
Run right, shoot everything. Terrain and enemies break apart.

3. Meet the quota
Built: every level has a kill quota. Kill enough (bullets, barrels, kamikaze blasts all count) and the extraction flag opens; touch it to move on. Three levels, then the run loops.
sfx: quota_reached

⛓

4. Rescue a prisoner
Free a caged Bro — you instantly swap to them (new weapon, new special). Also +1 life.

⬆

5. Pick an upgrade
End of level — choose 1 of 3 random perks (fire rate, double jump, bigger blasts...).

💀

6. Boss
Every 3rd level ends in a boss arena that can be torn apart.

↺

7. Die → start over
Built: three lives per run; a death restarts the level. Lose all lives and the run ends. Planned: unlocked Bros and meta-currency carry over to the next run.

Barrels & explosions

How blasts must read on screen — every size has its own look, and bigger piles must feel bigger.

1. Spot the barrel
Red drum with a skull, always placed near enemies. One block — you can stand on it.

2. Light it
One bullet sets it burning — flames on the lid, the drum flashes hot. Under half a second to get clear.
sfx: barrel_ignite

3. Single blast
One barrel = one small blast (fireball, starburst or plume — the same barrel always looks the same). Opens a hole about 2 blocks wide.
sfx: explosion_small

4. Starburst
The second small look — white rays first, then a ring of fire with sparks.

5. Plume
The third small look — a tall column that throws fire upward.

6. Cluster blast
Barrels touching each other go off as ONE blast — a mushroom fireball, bigger with every barrel, a small blast on each drum, more smoke, harder shake.
sfx: explosion_big

7. Shockwave
A ring races along the ground out to the cluster's full radius — that is exactly how far the damage reaches.

8. Chain reaction
Barrels further away that sit inside a blast catch fire and go off a moment later — piles ripple.

Enemies & blood

Each enemy has one job and one tell. Every kill is loud and messy, and the bodies stay.

1. Hit
Every bullet that lands sprays blood the way the bullet was flying (three different sprays, used in turn).

2. Kill
A death bursts (two kinds) and the body is thrown back.
sfx: blood_splat

3. Bodies stay
Where a body lands, a pool spreads under it and stays all level (three kinds). Blasts still toss bodies around.

4. Kamikaze spots you
Dynamite vest, lit stick. Same eyes as a mook — he only sees forward.

5. The charge
He screams and sprints at you, hopping walls. Up close he blows you both up — three hearts, a full-health Bro dies.
sfx: kamikaze_scream

6. Shoot him early
One bullet kills him — but his vest still goes off a third of a second later. Kill him far away, or next to his friends.

7. The Brute
Mini-boss, two blocks tall, two miniguns. Sees twice as far as a mook and spots you on platforms; hears you behind him.
sfx: brute_alert

8. Spin up
The barrels whine for most of a second before he fires — your cue to get behind something solid.
sfx: minigun_spinup

9. Spray
Long alternating bursts in a wide fan. They chew through cover and set off barrels — so lure him next to some.
sfx: minigun_fire
