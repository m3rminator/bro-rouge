Bro (default) player

The player character, exactly one block tall so he fits through a single destroyed block. Runs, jumps, sprays rifle fire; dies after max_hp enemy hits and the level restarts. Every future Bro copies this file and changes what makes them unique.

Mook (enemy soldier) enemy

Masked soldier. Patrols back and forth and turns at walls and ledges. He only sees forward — if the Bro is in front of him, within vision range, roughly at his height, with no blocks in between, he shows "!", waits reaction_time, then fires bursts. After losing sight of the Bro he keeps watching for forget_time, then patrols again. Shot from behind, he turns around.

Terrain block terrain

16×16 solid tile that levels are built from. Bullets (player and enemy) chip it; at 0 HP it bursts into debris and is gone for good. Blocks with open sky above (grass_clearance air tiles) automatically become grass with hanging strands; tunnel and cave floors stay bare earth. Each block's variant is picked from its map position, so the same level always looks the same.

Storyboards

Bro — moveset

The beats every Bro must sell. New Bros copy this board and change only what is unique to them.

1. Ready
Stands his ground, rifle up, bandana fluttering. Always looks ready to fight.

2. Run
Fast, forward-leaning sprint. Body bobs on each step, gun never lowers.
sfx: footstep

3. Jump
Tucked legs, bandana flips up. Jump is high and snappy, not floaty.
sfx: jump

4. Shoot
Gun kicks back 1px each shot, muzzle flash at the tip. Hold to spray.
sfx: shoot

5. Destroy
Bullets chew through dirt. Three hits and a block bursts into debris. The Bro is one block tall, so a single destroyed block is a tunnel he fits through.
sfx: block_break

6. Spotted
A mook only sees forward. Step in front of him, near his height, with nothing in between, and a "!" pops up — you have half a second.
sfx: enemy_alert

7. Firefight
He fires slow red bursts. Jump over them, or break the block under him. Two hits and he drops.
sfx: enemy_shoot

8. Hit
Each enemy hit costs a heart and makes the Bro blink (briefly invulnerable). Lose all three and the level restarts.
sfx: player_hurt

Run loop (roguelike)

What one run of the game feels like. This is the design target every feature serves.

🚁

1. Drop in
Helicopter drops a random Bro into a procedurally built jungle level.

2. Blast through
Run right, shoot everything. Terrain and enemies break apart.

⛓

3. Rescue a prisoner
Free a caged Bro — you instantly swap to them (new weapon, new special). Also +1 life.

⬆

4. Pick an upgrade
End of level — choose 1 of 3 random perks (fire rate, double jump, bigger blasts...).

💀

5. Boss
Every 3rd level ends in a boss arena that can be torn apart.

↺

6. Die → start over
Lose all lives and the run ends. Unlocked Bros and meta-currency carry over to the next run.
